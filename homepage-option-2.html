Questions People Ask Before Choosing Window Film

What is the difference between window film and window tinting?

Window tinting is a common term for film applied to glass, but window film is the broader category. It can include solar-control, heat-control, UV, privacy, security, decorative and specialty films—not just darker tint.

Does window film really reduce heat and glare?

Yes. Selected sun-control, solar-control and heat-control films are designed to reduce a portion of the solar energy entering through glass, while anti-glare films reduce excessive brightness and reflections. The right choice depends on the glass, exposure, desired light level and appearance.

Can window film help protect floors, furniture and artwork from fading?

Many sun-control and UV-filtering films reduce ultraviolet exposure through treated glass, which can help slow one contributor to fading. Heat, visible light, humidity, material age and other conditions can also contribute to fading.

Can I add privacy to existing glass without replacing it?

Yes. Depending on the space, options can include frosted or etched-look film, one-way reflective film, Smart Film, Casper Cloaking and blinds or shades. The best option depends on whether you need daytime, nighttime, permanent or switchable privacy.

Does security window film make glass unbreakable?

No. Security film does not make glass unbreakable. It is designed to help retain broken glass and may be used as part of a glazing system intended to increase resistance or delay entry. Performance also depends on the glass, attachment system and frame.

Can window film be installed on double-pane or Low-E windows?

Many films can be installed on insulated or Low-E glazing, but not every film is compatible with every window. The glass construction, coatings, film type and manufacturer requirements should be evaluated before installation.

How much does window film cost?

Pricing depends on the film, total glass area, number and size of panes, access, preparation, removal of existing film and installation conditions. A project-specific estimate is the best way to price the work accurately.

Do you install both residential and commercial window film?

Yes. Atlantic Sun Control works on residential, commercial, government, educational and other specialty projects, with product recommendations based on the property, glass and performance goal.

How do I know which window film I need?

Start with the problem, not the product name. Tell us whether you’re trying to reduce heat or glare, limit fading, create privacy, improve security, add graphics or solve another issue, and we can evaluate the property and recommend appropriate options.
